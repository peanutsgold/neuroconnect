Symptoms of brain injury

Symptoms vary from person to person and may not show up straight away. Many are “invisible” to others.

  • Headaches
  • Tiredness / fatigue
  • Dizziness
  • Nausea
  • Poor balance
  • Weakness on one side
  • Seizures
  • Sleep changes
  • Slurred speech
  • Memory problems
  • Poor concentration
  • Slower thinking
  • Trouble finding words
  • Difficulty planning
  • Confusion
  • Trouble making decisions
  • Feeling “foggy”
  • Irritability
  • Anxiety
  • Low mood / depression
  • Mood swings
  • Impulsiveness
  • Loss of motivation
  • Frustration
  • Changes in personality
  • Blurred or double vision
  • Sensitivity to light
  • Sensitivity to noise
  • Ringing in ears (tinnitus)
  • Changes in taste or smell
  • Numbness or tingling

⚠️ Get emergency help right away if someone has:

  • A headache that gets worse and won’t go away
  • Repeated vomiting
  • A seizure or convulsions
  • Unusual drowsiness or can’t be woken
  • One pupil larger than the other
  • Slurred speech, weakness, or numbness
  • Growing confusion, agitation, or odd behaviour
  • Clear fluid or blood from the nose or ears

Call 10177 or your local emergency number.
